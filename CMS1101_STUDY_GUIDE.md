## Quick Review Summaries

### Topic 1: Intro to Multimedia
- **Definition**: Computer information represented through audio, video, and animation in addition to traditional media (text, graphics, images).
- **Etymology**: Media is the plural of *medium* ("facilitating or linking communication").
- **Marshall McLuhan**: Famously stated *"The medium is the message"*.
- **5 Core Elements**:
  1. *Text*: Characters used to create words, sentences, paragraphs.
  2. *Graphics*: Digital representation of non-text info (drawings, charts, photos). Formats: Vector (formula-based, scalable) vs. Raster/Bitmap (pixel grid).
  3. *Animation*: Flipping through a series of still images to simulate movement.
  4. *Video*: Photographic visual frames captured from the real world.
  5. *Audio*: Spoken dialogue, music, and sound effects.
  - *Hypermedia*: An interactive structure where multimedia elements are linked non-linearly.
- **Categories**:
  - *Linear Multimedia*: Progresses without navigational control (e.g. standard cinema movie).
  - *Non-Linear (Interactive)*: User controls navigational progress (e.g. video games, CBT, hypermedia).
- **Digitization**: Analog signals are digitized through *sampling* (measuring at time intervals) and *quantization* (assigning discrete values).

### Topic 2: Color Theory & Psychology
- **Definition**: Color is the perceivable characteristic of light; light is energy, so color is a form of energy.
- **Color Wheel Hierarchy (12 Colors)**:
  - *Primary (3)*: Red, Yellow, Blue (cannot be created by mixing others).
  - *Secondary (3)*: Green, Orange, Purple (mixing two primaries).
  - *Tertiary (6)*: Primary + adjacent secondary (Yellow-Orange, Red-Orange, Red-Purple, Blue-Purple, Blue-Green, Yellow-Green).
- **Color Temperatures**:
  - *Warm*: Red, Orange, Yellow (passion, warmth, energy; visually advance).
  - *Cool*: Blue, Green, Purple (calm, serenity, trust; visually recede).
- **Harmonies**:
  - *Complementary*: Exact opposite on wheel (high vibrant contrast).
  - *Analogous*: Adjacent on wheel (harmonious, natural unity).
  - *Triadic*: 3 colors spaced evenly at 120° intervals.
  - *Monochromatic*: Single hue varied with tints (+white), shades (+black), and tones (+gray).
- **Color Models**:
  - *RGB (Red, Green, Blue)*: Additive light model for screens. Combining full intensity creates white.
  - *CMYK (Cyan, Magenta, Yellow, Key/Black)*: Subtractive pigment model for commercial printing.
- **Color Psychology Table**:
  - **Red**: Passion, love, urgency, appetite, danger, stop warnings.
  - **Blue**: Trust, calm, security, loyalty, professionalism, corporate reliability.
  - **Yellow**: Optimism, intellect, joy, sunshine; also caution/hazard warnings.
  - **Green**: Nature, ecology, health, renewal, wealth/money.
  - **Purple**: Royalty, luxury, wisdom, spirituality, mystery.
  - **Orange**: Vitality, fun, friendliness, creativity, affordability.
  - **Black**: Elegance, luxury, sophistication; but also mourning, death, evil.
  - **White**: Purity, cleanliness, neutrality, simplicity, spacious clarity.
